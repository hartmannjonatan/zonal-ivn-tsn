# C4 joint GCL/CBS sizing pilot — preregistered protocol

Status at creation: `exploratory=true`, `official_c4_frozen=false`, no final C4 campaign. This isolated study may change only pilot GCL schedules and pilot CBS reservations. P1, C0–C3, frozen C3 `reduction_11`, official files, source release schedule, routes, buffers, mapping, priorities, FIFO, PHY, no-preemption behavior, application clocks, oscillator drifts, and gPTP settings remain fixed.

## Questions and treatment space

The study separates gate cadence/window budget, relative phase, and permanent F13 recovery service. It uses local synchronized node clocks for every TAS gate. No ideal simulation clock, per-seed drift retuning, packet look-ahead, new CBS class, ATS, or credit-model change is allowed. F08–F10 retain SP with open gates on their three actual final egresses; transient-only zero-reservation TC4 egresses remain SP. TAS is limited to HPC→CSW and CSW→Z1/Z2/Z3; there is no TAS on `Z3.ZSW.eth[7]`.

Three preregistered GCL/CBS points span brake window versus TC4 service budget. Period is 100 µs in each local gate clock. TC7 opens at phase zero on HPC and at 0.772 µs on CSW egresses (84-byte brake MAC frame serialization plus 100 ns propagation). TC4 starts at `TC7_open − overlap` on HPC; CSW eth1/eth2 add serialization of a 1542-byte MAC frame plus 100 ns propagation, and CSW eth3 adds 84-byte serialization plus 100 ns. Offsets in INET are encoded as `(period − desired_start) mod period`. TC0–TC6 use the TC4 schedule; TC7 uses its brake schedule. Their overlaps are intentional and their open fractions are not exclusive service windows. Actual overlap differs by egress because of the forwarding-phase offsets: D1/D2 overlap is 10.000 µs on HPC, 11.664 µs on CSW eth1/eth2, and 10.000 µs on CSW eth3; D3 is 12.000 µs on all four. These exact phases and offsets are in `results/dimensioning.json` and `treatment_contracts.json`.

The four rates are generated from the frozen C3 L2 profile and measured A005 queue-bit demands. For each egress, the fixed formula is `ceil(1.20 × max(historical queue-bit offer, permanent catalog queue-bit rate) / eligible_fraction)`, where `eligible_fraction = (TC4 open − circular TC4/TC7 overlap − 12.4 µs maximum-frame fit guard − 1 µs relative-phase allowance)/100 µs`. The 12.4 µs guard is `(1542×8 + 64)/1 Gb/s`, matching installed INET's `extraLength=64 b` packet-fit check. The 1 µs allowance is an engineering envelope aligned to the sampled offset criterion, not proof of continuous synchronization. Relative oscillator drift per 100 µs cycle and corrections are recorded; they are not used for seed-specific gate changes.

This formula is a sizing estimate, not a service guarantee. It targets 20% headroom over the higher queue-bit demand, excludes the 53-transient contribution from permanent reservations, and separately records permanent `R_wire`. It does not prove a positive lower service bound because competition, credit availability, gate-fit, and clock correction timing remain empirical. Candidates with a 75% aggregate port-cap failure or an optimistic no-guard upper service below measured demand would be rejected without simulation; all three generated candidates pass the cap screen and have no known hard upper-bound rejection. Candidate estimates, cap sums and all C3/A005 ratios are in `results/dimensioning.json`.

The F13 change is fixed at `Z3.ZSW.eth[7]/TC4 = 1.02 × A005 = 28.152 Mb/s` for all D candidates. It is an exploratory local recovery adjustment, not a general calibration.

## Controls, comparison, and selection

First use L2/A1 seed 41. Reuse corrected `B_local` and `C3_sync_control` only after the independent equivalence audit recorded in `plan.json` passes. Run each candidate pair in order: `D1_TAS`, `D1_noTAS`, `D2_TAS`, `D2_noTAS`, `D3_TAS`, `D3_noTAS`. Each noTAS control has identical CBS reservations and gPTP, with empty periodic schedules. B is A005 with its local GCL; C3 is frozen `reduction_11` with no TAS.

Apply P1 application and stability gates without threshold changes. Separately report technical validity, analytic admissibility, application gates, stability, sampled synchronization, continuous synchronization (not verified unless directly supported), and service evidence. A candidate is eligible for selection only if it passes all applicable P1 gates and has sufficient service evidence. Select the passing candidate with the smallest aggregate increase above C3, normalized by physical port rate; ties are resolved D1, D2, D3. Its noTAS arm identifies the effect of gates at equal CBS; `D_i_TAS − C3` is the combined TAS and CBS-resizing effect.

For F13, identify natural episodes at more than one 2048-bit queue frame above pre-episode baseline. Recovery is return to baseline +2048 bits within 5 s, while the source remains active, with zero confirmed drops. Five seconds is one P1 stability aggregation interval used as a diagnostic horizon, not a new P1 gate. Report `Q(1−)`, `Q(31−)`, queue-resident packets, in-service packet, arrivals, removals and drops with common event order. If there is no natural episode, recovery is `not_identifiable`. Throughput equality with empty endpoints is conservation, not evidence of available margin.

If a candidate otherwise passes P1 but natural recovery and analytical bounds remain non-identifying, a separate two-run artificial-backlog diagnostic is authorized: selected TAS and its noTAS control, both at L2/A1 seed41; initialize F13 with 53 observed historical frames while its source remains active; evaluate return to within one frame of baseline by 5 s and zero drops. This does not join workload comparisons or establish P1 compliance. It is not run unless that condition occurs and an auditable queue-injection mechanism is available without changing the model's CBS behavior.

If selected, keep the configuration fixed for paired checks: candidate TAS, its noTAS control, and C3_sync at L2/A1 seeds43/47; then the same three treatments at L0/A0 and L1/A1 seed41. Derive each load's CBS from that load's frozen C3 profile using the same ratios for the four gate-affected TC4 reservations and the +2% relative local Z3 eth7 adjustment; do not scale transient flows into permanent demand. No retuning follows new results. If no candidate passes/has service evidence in seed41, stop; no conditional runs.

Budget: six new candidate/control runs plus reused B/C3 in the initial set; at most 6 additional paired seed checks and 6 load checks; maximum 20 principal treatments. Two technical replacements maximum, recorded separately; performance failure is never a replacement reason. Jobs=1. Stop before starting a run if free disk <40 GiB or `MemAvailable` <7 GiB, and terminate a running simulator if either floor is crossed. Retain full vectors compressed after verified processing, compact traces, calendar, SCA/log, gates, credits, queue balances and exact manifests. Never remove historical or official data.
