Recorded after the six preregistered L2/A1 seed-41 runs and trace reprocessing. The preregistered protocol and selection threshold are unchanged. All six methodological runs were technically valid and paired to the same F21/F22 calendar. All application gates, the repository stability proxy, GCL checks (where applicable), and sampled synchronization checks passed. Continuous synchronization compliance remains unverified.
